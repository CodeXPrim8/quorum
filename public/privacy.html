Privacy policy

CLEMX TECHNOLOGIES LTD. Effective 12 November 2026.

Quorom Si (QSI) is a company operating system at www.quorom.ng. This policy explains what the service collects and how CLEMX TECHNOLOGIES LTD uses it. The operator’s contact address is iwewezinemstephen@gmail.com.

Account and company information

When you create an account, we store your email address and a sign-in credential through our authentication provider. When you set up a company, we store the company name, your name and title, the departments and roles you select, and the website or social links you choose to enter. We use this information to run the company workspace you asked for.

Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp

If you connect Instagram or WhatsApp, you sign in with Meta and grant the permissions shown on Meta’s screen. We receive the identifiers Meta returns for the Facebook Page, Instagram account, and WhatsApp phone number you select, together with the access tokens needed to send and receive messages and to publish content you approve.

Message content that arrives on the connected WhatsApp number, and the name and phone number of the person who sent it, is stored so your company can read it and reply inside Quorom Si. We do not sell this information. We do not use it to advertise to those people on other sites.

Meta processes the same connection under its own terms. You can remove the connection inside Quorom Si with Disconnect, and you can remove the app in your Facebook settings.

How long we keep it

Account, company, and message records stay while the company workspace exists. Access tokens are stored in encrypted form and are deleted when you disconnect that channel.

Deletion and questions

To delete your account, company workspace, or connected Meta data, email iwewezinemstephen@gmail.com from the address on the account. Say whether you want the account, the company workspace, WhatsApp messages, or the Instagram connection removed. We delete the requested records from the service unless we must keep a copy to meet a legal duty.
